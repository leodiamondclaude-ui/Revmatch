# studio-car.glb

"Car Concept" from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets
(https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept),
licensed **CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It was itself derived from a
public-domain (CC0) model by Unity Fan on Sketchfab.

Changes made for RevMatch: removed the licence-plate and steering-emblem meshes and all Khronos logo
textures (logos are excluded from the CC BY grant), removed material variants, converted textures
to WebP, and applied meshopt compression. Paint, rim, caliper and glass materials are recoloured at
runtime.
